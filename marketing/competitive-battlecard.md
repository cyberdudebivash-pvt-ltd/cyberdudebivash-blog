# Competitive Battle Card — Recorded Future & GreyNoise

Internal sales-enablement reference. Every competitor fact below is sourced
to a specific, cited public source (checked directly, not asserted from
memory) — see citations at the bottom. Re-verify before quoting in a live
deal if this document is more than a few months old; competitor pricing and
features change without notice and this file will not update itself.

## vs. Recorded Future

| Dimension | Recorded Future | Sentinel APEX |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing model | Not independently price-checked; enterprise-motion positioning (public-sector offering exists) | Fully public, self-serve, four fixed tiers from $0 to ~$60/mo |
| Scale | Claims 900B data points/day, 13B+ entity Intelligence Graph, 430 nation-state-sponsored groups tracked | Materially smaller graph — do not claim comparable scale, it is not true |
| Report production | Largely automated intelligence production at scale | Small number of hand-authored, evidence-cited, individually certified reports — depth and traceability per report, not volume |
| Compliance posture | Public-sector-specific offering suggests compliance investment we have not made | None claimed — do not imply otherwise |

**How to position:** Do not compete on scale or automation volume — that
comparison loses. Compete on: (1) transparent self-serve pricing where
enterprise vendors typically require a sales call to learn cost, and (2) per-
report evidence traceability (every ATT&CK mapping cites the specific
sentence that supports it) as a different, complementary quality claim to
"we ingest more data."

## vs. GreyNoise

| Dimension | GreyNoise | Sentinel APEX |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing model | No public pricing — tier + module quote-only | Fully public pricing, all four tiers |
| Product focus | Internet-scan/attack-traffic classification (IP Lookup, GNQL, RIOT, CVE, Sessions, Recall, IP Timeline), plus an MCP server for AI workflows | Broader: live actor/campaign/CVE graph + hand-authored analyst reports, not primarily a scan-classification product |
| Free tier | Community tier exists | Free tier exists |
| Real-time capability | "Real-Time Threat Defense" launched publicly (Black Hat 2025) | 30-minute ingestion cycle — do not claim "real-time" without qualification; state the actual cycle time |

**How to position:** Our clearest, honestly-claimable edge against GreyNoise
specifically is pricing transparency — they have none published, we have
all four tiers public. Do not claim feature parity on scan-traffic
classification; that is not our product's focus.

## Universal rules for any competitive conversation

1. Never state a competitor's price, feature, or customer count that isn't
   sourced below or independently re-verified first.
2. Never claim graph/data scale parity with Recorded Future — it is false
   and would be caught immediately by any technical buyer who checks.
3. Lead with pricing transparency and per-report evidence traceability —
   both are true, verifiable, and differentiated.
4. If asked about compliance certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, etc.), say
   plainly that none are currently held — do not deflect or imply otherwise.
