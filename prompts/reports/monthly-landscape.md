# REPORT TYPE — MONTHLY THREAT LANDSCAPE

**Version:** 1.0 · **Layer 3** · Inherits Constitution + Production Workflow
**Use when:** producing the recurring monthly strategic landscape report — the
flagship subscription artifact.

---

## Objective
Deliver the strategic, data-grounded picture of the month: what shifted, what the
trends mean, and where to invest defensive effort next. This is the report that
most justifies an annual subscription — it must contain **original analysis and
data**, not a summary of summaries.

## Principles
- **Data-driven.** Ground trends in reproducible data (e.g., the Exploitation
  Velocity Index: KEV additions, vendor concentration, ransomware share,
  remediation-window urgency). Cite the dataset and method.
- **Strategic, not reactive.** Explain direction and significance over a month,
  not individual incidents.
- **Falsifiable claims.** Every trend statement is backed by a number a reader
  can reproduce from the cited source.

## Section structure
1. **Executive Landscape Summary** — the month in a paragraph a board would read.
2. **By the Numbers** — KEV additions this month, top affected vendors,
   ransomware-linked share, notable remediation deadlines — from primary data,
   with method noted.
3. **Trend Analysis** — 3–5 trends, each with supporting data and a "so what" for
   defenders. Distinguish signal from noise.
4. **Threat Actor & Ransomware Movement** — evidence-backed shifts in activity.
5. **AI Security Landscape** — the month's meaningful AI-security developments,
   framework-mapped.
6. **Sector Spotlight** — one industry's month (optionally apply the Industry
   Overlay), with sector-specific implications.
7. **Strategic Recommendations** — where to focus effort/budget next month, tied
   to the data.
8. **Future Outlook** — labeled forward assessment, with what would change it.
9. **Intelligence Gaps** · **Methodology & Sources** · **Analyst Conclusion.**

## Do not
- Assert trends without data. · Recycle the weekly digests. · Present a
  correlation as causation. · Publish a "landscape" that is just a link roundup.
